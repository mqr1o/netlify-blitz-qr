Blitz QR is a free QR code generator with logo support. Make QR codes for links, Wi‑Fi networks, email and phone numbers, add your own logo, an icon or a monogram, and export a 2048 px PNG or an SVG for print. Everything runs in your browser, so nothing you type is uploaded.
